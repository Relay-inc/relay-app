These repos have a pull request template, the path in your worktree:
{{templates}}
For each of them you change, read its template and fill in every section for your change, with its headings and checklist as they are (tick only what you did or checked). A section you cannot fill gets n/a with the reason, for example screenshots when you could not run the app. Never delete a section. Put each filled template in report's `pr_body`, under the repo's name as listed here. Relay uses it as that PR's description instead of your summary, and adds the ticket, the other repos, the checks, the review notes and your decisions after it, so leave those out.
