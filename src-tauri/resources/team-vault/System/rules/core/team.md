# Team

Fill this in. Claude reads it every session, for every member. The setup skill fills it in through a short interview.

## What this vault is for
One or two lines: what the team keeps here, and what it does not.

## Who works in it
One line per member: name, role, what they mostly work on.

## How the team works together
Who keeps what up to date, shared conventions (one note per company, a contact log, naming), how a handover between members goes.

## People and organizations
The external people and organizations that matter most, one line each.

## Never write here
What Claude must keep out of this vault beyond the hard walls: personal data of members, secrets, topics that belong elsewhere.
