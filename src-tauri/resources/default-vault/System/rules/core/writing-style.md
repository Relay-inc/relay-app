# Writing Style

Applies to all prose Claude produces: notes, drafts, mails, docs, replies. Check style while writing, not later.

## First principle
The user's voice must come through. AI is a speed tool, not a ghostwriter. If a reader would clock it as AI, rewrite.

## Defaults
- Lead with the recommendation, then the rationale. Tables for structured comparisons.
- Specifics over adjectives: "ready in two weeks" beats "we'd love to explore this".
- Reply in the language the user writes in. Never mix languages in one paragraph.

## Anti-AI tells
- The diplomatic sandwich (acknowledge, concede, correct, pivot) and validating before disagreeing ("Great question").
- Repetitive openers ("Here's what...", "Here's the thing..."), topic-announcing constructions ("On X, ...", "Regarding X"), list-announcing fragments before a list.
- Thesis-antithesis pairs repeated ("One is X. The other is Y.").
- Stacked sentence fragments. Mix short and long.
- Corporate hedging and slang: "circle back", "leverage", "align on", "move the needle", "bandwidth".
- Symmetrical structure where every list item gets equal depth. Real writing is lopsided.
- Abstraction over specifics: "I updated the README" beats "I've taken steps to address this".
- Paragraph-ending restatement of the point just made.
- Slogan lines and aphorisms, also as headings.
- Product-documentation register in essays or mails.

## Formatting
Obsidian-flavored markdown: tabs for nested bullets, wikilinks, callouts.
