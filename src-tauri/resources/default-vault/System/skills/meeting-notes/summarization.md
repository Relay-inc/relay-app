# Summarization Accuracy

Rules for any summarization: conversation notes, status rewrites, and ad-hoc summaries of a mail or a document.

## Keep Threads Distinct
- Separate discussion topics stay separate. If two concerns came up about the same product, they get two topic sections (or two bullets), not one merged statement.
- Test: could a reader tell these were discussed independently? If not, split them.

## Specific Attribution
- Name the artifact, survey, document, or data source, not a vague category.
  - Bad: "based on survey feedback"
  - Good: "based on the Q4 customer survey results"
  - When the source is genuinely unknown from the notes: "based on customer feedback (source unspecified)"
- When raw notes are vague, preserve the vagueness rather than sharpening with inferred specifics.

## No Inference Bridging
- Do not connect topics the source material does not explicitly connect.
- Two things discussed in the same meeting are not necessarily related.
- Compression should lose detail, not add interpretation.

## Uncertainty Over Plausibility
- When raw notes are ambiguous, summarize what was said, not what was probably meant.
- Flag gaps openly: "(unclear from notes whether X relates to Y)" beats silently merging.
